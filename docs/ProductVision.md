# Travel Planner – Product Vision

## Product Vision

The Travel Planner application will provide travelers with one place to organize and manage important information related to their trips. Instead of keeping flight, hotel, transportation, activity, and personal travel information in separate places, users will be able to access their travel plans through a single application.

The application is intended for people planning vacations or business trips. It will allow users to organize trip details, compare travel options, and refer back to information from previous trips.

## Key Features

- Store user and travel information.
- Create trip itineraries containing activities and events.
- Find transportation options, including bus and train routes.
- Find nearby hotels and view pricing information.
- Find flight options and view flight details.
- Save trip information for future reference.
